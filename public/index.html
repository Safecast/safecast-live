SAFECAST LIVE / V2.0ENVIRONMENTAL SIGNAL PROCESSOR
Waiting for location

    Paused

    An entirely random, environmental, generative soundscape. This work is driven by a global, real time network of radiation and air quality sensors. As data is received, readings are mapped to specific samples. Ranges have different tonal markers and it's possible to recognize higher or lower measurements by sound alone. GPS maps to the specific location of the sensor, imagery however is representative of the general area because it looks better in this artistic context. 2026 - v2.0
